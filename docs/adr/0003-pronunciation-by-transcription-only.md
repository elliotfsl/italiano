# Pronunciation is judged by what Scribe transcribed, no pronunciation service

ElevenLabs Scribe (language pinned to Italian) transcribes the spoken reply. If the
transcript matches the intended words, pronunciation passed. If a word came through as a
different word, that word is flagged and replayed in the Italian voice. No phoneme scoring
service (Azure Speech was the candidate) is used, by Elliot's decision on metà settembre: the
errors that block him are register and `ne`, not accent, and the transcript catches accent
exactly when it would confuse a real listener.
