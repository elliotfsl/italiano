Built for: Elliot, A1/A2, Italy, mid-September. Last built: 2026-07-18.

Content banks and phase specs for the program. The session runner is `PROTOCOL.md`; it reads this file every session. Sessions tick scenario checkboxes here (`- [x] (gNN)`); the giorno-1 session fills Contesto viaggio; nothing else in this file changes during the program.

## Le cinque fasi

Dates are computed on giorno 1 (compression rule in PROTOCOL.md section 8) and live in STATUS.md. Nominal lengths below.

### Fase 1: Sbloccare (7 giorni)
Goal: 30 sustained minutes in Italian without panic; introduce himself and the group; order at a bar.
- Language surfaced just-in-time: present tense of essere, avere, andare, volere, potere, fare, prendere, mangiare; survival chunks (vorrei, mi scusi, quanto costa, dov'è, non ho capito, può ripetere, più lentamente per favore); numbers, prices, times.
- Conversations: himself, his day, his people, why Italy, the trip outline.
- Pressione menu: describe-don't-name with everyday objects; rapid-fire sì/no then short answers.
- Exit check (end of phase, informal): he can hold the warm-up entirely in Italian and ask for repetition without switching to English.

### Fase 2: Passato e programmi (14 giorni)
Goal: talk about yesterday and about plans; survive first role-plays.
- Language surfaced: passato prossimo through daily "cosa hai fatto ieri" talk (avere vs essere aux as it comes up); plans as chunks (vorrei + infinito, ho intenzione di, penso di, si può); future forms only as formulas (sarà, andremo).
- First role-plays from the banks: bar breakfast, simple restaurant, buying tickets. Repair phrases must become automatic here.
- Pressione menu: "racconta ieri in 5 frasi"; mild complications (one wrinkle per scene).
- Exit check: tells a 5-sentence story about yesterday without English; orders for the group.

### Fase 3: Scenario gauntlet (17 giorni)
Goal: handle all four scenario categories WITH complications; start real phone calls.
- Language surfaced: imperfetto through storytelling ("com'era?"); direct object pronouns as they block him; quantities and measures for markets.
- Every main conversation is a role-play with a built-in complication (the banks below carry them). Chiamate vere rehearsals begin per the table.
- Pressione menu: phone mode (no visual cues, realistic operator speed); complication stacking (two wrinkles); circumlocution under time pressure.
- Exit check: completes a phone-mode booking start to finish, no English.

### Fase 4: Velocità e sfumature (14 giorni)
Goal: natural speed, opinions, listening asymmetry; Sardinia flavor.
- Language surfaced: colloquial fillers (allora, magari, boh, cioè, insomma, dai); opinions (secondo me, mi sembra, preferisco perché); enough conditional to be polite (sarebbe possibile).
- Listening asymmetry: your turns get longer and faster than what you require back.
- Sardinia flavor notes, color not curriculum: Italian works everywhere; Sardu exists and Alghero speaks a Catalan variety; "ajò" means come on; menu literacy: seadas, culurgiones, porceddu, fregola, pane carasau, bottarga, mirto, Cannonau, Vermentino.
- Boat-call rehearsals land here per the table (weather and Maestrale wind questions included).
- Pressione menu: rapid opinion pivots; "convincimi" mini-debates; fast-speaker simulation.
- Exit check: holds a real opinion exchange at near-natural speed and repairs breakdowns in Italian.

### Fase 5: Simulazione (8 giorni, ends metà settembre)
Goal: full days in Italy, chained scenarios, Italian only.
- Italian-only sessions; the "?" hatch gives a one-line gloss and gets counted.
- Full-day simulations of his REAL itinerary days, chaining 4-6 scenes: arrival day (rental desk at Olbia, drive questions, lunch on the lungomare, apartment check-in, dinner); boat day (booking confirmation, port, onboard chat, agriturismo dinner); Alghero day (parking and ZTL, old town, market, special dinner); La Maddalena day; a Sicily evening.
- Stress tests: fast speaker, being misunderstood, problem stacking, noisy-phone mode.
- Final benchmark (run 4) per the table in STATUS. Generate and refresh `frasario.md` (PROTOCOL section 10).

## Benchmark fissi

Three scenarios, run identically on giorno 1, first session from g20, from g40, and within 5 days of departure. Frozen rules, they OVERRIDE phase policy: Italian only, zero glosses, recasts allowed but no teaching, "?" allowed and counted, 4-5 minutes per scenario, same order, deliver the scripted lines verbatim. If he freezes, one scripted fallback re-prompt: "Prego, mi dica pure." Save the full transcript plus the scoring block into the log immediately after the segment, before the debrief.

### Scenario 1: Al ristorante (cibo)
Scene: Friday night, a busy seafood place, he speaks for the group of five.
- Opener (verbatim): "Buonasera! Benvenuti. Siete in cinque? ... Ecco il menù. Posso portarvi qualcosa da bere?"
- Mid complication (verbatim): "Mi dispiace, il pesce spada stasera è finito. Posso consigliarle la fregola con le arselle. Qualcuno di voi ha allergie o intolleranze?"
- Closer (verbatim): "Come preferite il conto, unico o separato?"

### Scenario 2: Banco autonoleggio a Olbia (trasporti)
Scene: just landed, jet-lagged, picking up the SUV.
- Opener (verbatim): "Buongiorno, benvenuto a Olbia! Ha una prenotazione? Mi dà patente e carta di credito, per favore?"
- Mid complication (verbatim): "C'è un piccolo problema: serve la patente internazionale insieme a quella americana. Ce l'ha? ... Le consiglio anche la copertura completa, solo venti euro al giorno. La vuole?"
- Closer (verbatim): "Perfetto. Dove andate oggi? ... Mi raccomando, attenzione alle zone a traffico limitato nei centri storici. Buon viaggio!"

### Scenario 3: Small talk al bar (socialità)
Scene: morning coffee at the counter, a friendly local starts talking.
- Opener (verbatim): "Scusi, Lei non è di qui, vero? Di dov'è? ... E cosa La porta in Sardegna?"
- Mid complication, delivered fast (verbatim): "Secondo me settembre è il mese migliore, altro che agosto: meno gente e il mare è ancora caldo. Lei che ne pensa? Preferisce le città o il mare?"
- Closer (verbatim): "Se avete una sera libera vi consiglio un posto io. E Lei, mi consiglia qualcosa della mia città?"

### Scoring block (identical every run)

```
## Punteggi (benchmark N, giorno NN)
| Scenario | Compito | "?" | Riparazioni | Compr. | Sciolt. | Corr. | Lessico |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ristorante | sì/no | n | n | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 |
| Autonoleggio | sì/no | n | n | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 |
| Bar | sì/no | n | n | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 |

Citazioni (2-3 sue frasi, verbatim):
- "..."

Giudizio complessivo (un paragrafo, onesto):
...

Confronto col benchmark precedente (salta al run 1):
...
```

Definitions, so scores stay comparable: Compito = did he complete the transaction without you rescuing him in English. Riparazioni = times he asked for repetition or restated to fix a breakdown (this going UP early is good, then down late). Compr. = how much he understood at delivery speed. Sciolt. = flow and turn length. Corr. = grammar and form, dictation artifacts excluded. Lessico = range and precision.
